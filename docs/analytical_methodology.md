# Analytical Methodology

## 1. Analytical Objective

The project evaluates the Zomato menu portfolio across four connected
dimensions:

1.  Pricing position
2.  Customer engagement through menu-item votes
3.  Restaurant / cuisine / city performance
4.  Portfolio strategy

The intended analytical progression is:

**Menu item → Price Tier + Popularity Segment → Product Strategy →
Market intelligence → Strategic action**

## 2. Analytical Questions

-   Which menu items attract the strongest customer engagement?
-   How does price positioning relate to customer demand?
-   Which products represent scalable volume opportunities?
-   Which premium products demonstrate established demand?
-   Where is the portfolio carrying dormant products?
-   Which cities, cuisines and restaurants provide useful performance
    benchmarks?
-   Where should premiumization, optimization or rationalization be
    prioritized?

## 3. Segmentation Framework

### Price Tier

The project uses four business-facing price tiers:

-   Budget: ≤ ₹150
-   Value: ₹151--300
-   Premium: ₹301--500
-   Luxury: \> ₹500

### Popularity Segment

Popularity is classified using customer vote volume:

-   Dormant Products: 0
-   Niche Products: 1--15
-   Popular Products: 16--50
-   High-Performing Products: 51--100
-   Signature Products: 101+

The thresholds were established through distribution analysis, histogram
evaluation and percentile assessment, with business interpretability
used as a final consideration.

### Product Strategy Segment

The project combines price position and popularity into four strategic
roles:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Price                   Popularity              Strategy
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Budget / Value          Popular /               Volume Drivers
                          High-Performing /       
                          Signature               

  Premium / Luxury        Popular /               Premium Winners
                          High-Performing /       
                          Signature               

  Premium / Luxury        Dormant / Niche         Dormant Premium

  Budget / Value          Dormant / Niche         Dormant Core
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

This creates a portfolio-management lens rather than a simple popularity
ranking.

## 4. Multi-Dimensional Analysis

The resulting strategy segments are analyzed by:

-   City
-   Cuisine
-   Restaurant
-   Price Tier
-   Customer engagement
-   Premium Winner concentration
-   Restaurant engagement efficiency

This makes it possible to distinguish between a product that is popular
because of broad volume and a product that is successful despite premium
pricing.

## 5. Decision Framework

The project translates segments into four broad actions:

-   **Scale** --- proven Volume Drivers and Premium Winners
-   **Optimize** --- products with weaker performance but potential for
    repositioning
-   **Premiumize** --- categories and markets where demand already
    supports higher price positioning
-   **Rationalize** --- Dormant Core and Dormant Premium products with
    limited engagement

## 6. Analytical Principle

The project deliberately avoids treating a single metric as a complete
measure of product success. Ratings, price, votes, restaurant context
and market context are considered together before recommendations are
made.
